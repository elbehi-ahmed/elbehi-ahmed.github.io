Engineer in training, security‑minded by default.

I'm a 3rd-year Networking & Telecommunications Engineering student at the Institut National des Sciences Appliquées et de Technologie (INSAT) in Tunis. My focus is cybersecurity — I like going down to the bits: implementing hash functions from scratch in C, reproducing attacks on RSA, and solving CTF challenges with the Securinets INSAT club.
